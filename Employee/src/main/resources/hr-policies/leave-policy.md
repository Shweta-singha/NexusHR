# Leave Policy

## Leave Types

NexusHR employees accrue leave under four categories:

- **Casual Leave (CASUAL)** — 12 days per year. Does not carry forward; unused
  casual leave lapses at year-end.
- **Sick Leave (SICK)** — 12 days per year. Does not carry forward.
- **Earned Leave (EARNED)** — 24 days per year, accrued monthly. Carries
  forward into the next year, capped at 10 days carried forward per
  year-end cycle. Any earned leave balance above the 10-day cap is forfeited
  at the year-end close.
- **Compensatory Off (COMP_OFF)** — up to 10 days per year, granted for
  approved overtime or weekend work. Does not carry forward.

## Applying for Leave

1. Submit a leave request through the Leave module, specifying the leave
   type, start date, and end date.
2. A submitted request moves to Pending status and is routed to your
   manager or an HR Manager for approval.
3. Approved leave deducts the requested days from your balance for that
   leave type. Rejected or cancelled requests do not affect your balance.
4. Leave balance checks and approvals are subject to available balance —
   you cannot apply for more days than your remaining balance in that
   category.

## Carry-Forward and Year-End Close

Earned Leave carry-forward is processed automatically at year-end. Balances
above the 10-day carry-forward cap are not paid out and do not roll over.
Casual, Sick, and Comp Off balances reset to zero and are not paid out.
