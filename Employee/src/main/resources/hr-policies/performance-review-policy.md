# Performance Management Policy

## Goals

Employees set goals for each review cycle through the Performance module,
each with a title, description, and target date. Goal status tracks
progress (Not Started, In Progress, Completed) and is visible to the
employee's manager.

## Performance Reviews

Reviews are authored by a manager or HR Manager and record a 1–5 rating
along with written comments for a given review period. A rating of 1–2
indicates significant concerns, 3 indicates meets expectations, and 4–5
indicates exceeds expectations.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

An employee who receives a rating of 1–2, or whose manager identifies a
sustained performance gap, may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan.
A PIP defines specific, measurable goals and a review checkpoint, typically
30–90 days out. Failure to meet PIP goals by the checkpoint may lead to
further disciplinary action, up to and including termination, per the Code
of Conduct's disciplinary process.

## Review Cadence

Formal reviews are conducted at least annually; many teams also run
informal mid-cycle check-ins. Attrition risk scoring (visible to HR
Managers) is informational only and does not directly factor into review
ratings.
