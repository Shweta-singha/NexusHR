# Remote Work / Work-From-Home Policy

## Eligibility

All confirmed employees are eligible for remote work arrangements unless
their role requires physical presence (e.g. front-desk or facilities
roles). New joiners typically work on-site for their first 90 days before
becoming eligible for remote work.

## Standard Arrangement

- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week without prior approval,
  coordinated with their manager.
- Fully remote arrangements (more than 2 days per week) require written
  manager approval and are reviewed quarterly.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM–4:00 PM local time, during which
  employees are expected to be reachable regardless of work location.

## Equipment and Expenses

Company-issued laptops remain the employee's responsibility while working
remotely. Home-office equipment (monitor, chair, etc.) can be claimed
through the expense reimbursement process, subject to the annual home-office
allowance and manager approval.

## Attendance Tracking

Remote work days are still logged through the Attendance module like any
other work day. Employees should mark attendance at the start of their
working day regardless of location.
