# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses

Employees may claim reimbursement for:

- Client-related travel (economy airfare/train, local transport, lodging)
- Home-office equipment, up to the annual home-office allowance referenced
  in the Remote Work Policy
- Work-related training, certifications, or conference fees pre-approved by
  a manager
- Client entertainment, within department-set limits

Personal expenses, commute to a regular office location, and unapproved
purchases are not reimbursable.

## Submission Process

Expense claims are submitted with itemized receipts to the employee's
manager for approval, then routed to Payroll for reimbursement in the next
payroll cycle. Claims submitted without receipts, or more than 60 days
after the expense date, may be rejected.

## Limits and Approval

- Claims under 5,000 require only manager approval.
- Claims of 5,000 or more require both manager and HR Manager approval.
- Travel expenses should follow the lowest reasonable-cost option; premium
  travel requires prior written approval.
