# Code of Conduct

## Workplace Behavior

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, managers, and reports with
professionalism and respect. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of
any kind is prohibited and should be reported to HR immediately through the
Admin module's contact channel or directly to an HR Manager.

## Confidentiality

Employee records, payroll data, and performance review content accessed
through NexusHR are confidential. Employees with elevated access (HR
Manager, Admin roles) must not share salary, PF/ESI, or personal data
outside of their authorized use.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or
personal relationship that could create a conflict of interest with their
role, particularly where it intersects with hiring, payroll, or performance
decisions.

## Disciplinary Process

Conduct violations are handled through a progressive process: informal
counseling, a documented warning recorded against the employee's record,
and — for serious or repeated violations — escalation to a Performance
Improvement Plan or termination, at HR Manager/Admin discretion.
